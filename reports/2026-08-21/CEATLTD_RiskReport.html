  • CEAT Limited manufactures automotive tyres, tubes, and flaps in India and internationally..
  • The company offers a range of tyres for rubber tires and tubes for scooters, bikes, two and three wheelers, cars, buses, LCVs, trucks, and tractors, as well as agricultural and industrial radial tyres..
  • It also exports its products..
  • The company was formerly known as CEAT Tyres of India Limited and changed its name to CEAT Limited in 1990..
