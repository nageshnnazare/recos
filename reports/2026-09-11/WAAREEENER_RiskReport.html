🏢 Company Analysis · Waaree Energies Limited
💼 BUSINESS MODEL
  • Waaree Energies Limited manufactures and trades in solar photovoltaic modules in India and internationally..
  • It operates through Solar Photovoltaic Modules; Generation of Power; and Engineering, Procurement and Construction Contract segments..
  • The company offers PV modules, such as HJT, N Type, mono PERC, flexible, and special modules; single and three phase on-grid inverter; solar kits; battery energy storage system; electrolyzer stacks; smart meter; transformer; and Waaree lights..
  • It also provides engineering, procurement, and construction services; asset management; and solar systems for emerging, hybrid, and niche formats, including tracker-integrated systems, agrovoltaics, BIPV, and repowering legacy plants..
  • Operates in Solar within the Technology sector.
  • Workforce of 2,110 employees.
