💼 BUSINESS MODEL
  • Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells lead-acid storage batteries for industrial and automotive applications in India and internationally..
  • It operates in two segments, Lead Acid Batteries and Allied Products; and New Energy Business..
  • The company provides new energy business related products; installation, commissioning, and maintenance services; and industrial chargers, integrated power systems, electric charging solutions, and battery energy storage systems, as well as lithium-based home UPS systems and hybrid inverter platforms for residential and small commercial applications..
  • It also develops, manufactures, and sells energy storage and management products, solutions, and technologies related to lithium cells, advanced cell chemistries, battery packs, and components..
  • Operates in Electrical Equipment & Parts within the Industrials sector.
