🏢 Company Analysis · GE Vernova T&D India Limited
💼 BUSINESS MODEL
  • GE Vernova T&D India Limited engages in power transmission and distribution business in India and internationally..
  • It offers grid solutions for generation, transmission, distribution, and industries and infrastructures, such as HVDC/FACTS, AC substations solution and services for system integration; HV/MV equipment, including circuit breakers, disconnectors, instrument transformers, power transformers, capacitors, and reactors; and automation protection comprising digital substations, asset performance and energy management..
  • The company also provides power conversion and storage for marine, oil and gas, power, hybrid power evacuation, distribution, and electro-intensive industries consisting of motors, generators, and induction and synchronous rotating machineries; low-voltage and medium-voltage power electronics drives; and automation, controls, and digital, which include microgrids, asset performance management, and energy management systems..
  • In addition, it offers FlexInverter, a solar inverter; FlexReservoir, a battery system; FlexIQ, plantification, and asset performance management digital solutions; converters, switchgear, and protection and control solutions and services; and propulsion, generation, distribution, and power quality solutions and services..
