💼 BUSINESS MODEL
  • Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited engages in the building and repairing of ships, submarines, vessels, and related engineering products in India and internationally..
  • The company operates in two segments, Shipbuilding, and Submarine and Heavy Engineering..
  • The company offers cargo ships, passenger ships, supply vessels, multipurpose support vessels, water tankers, tugs, dredgers, fishing trawlers, barges, and border outposts..
  • It also provides services, including repairs and refit, designing of ships, know how provider, training, tug support, supply chain management, and self-propeller modular transporter..
